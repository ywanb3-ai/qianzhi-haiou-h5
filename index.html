关于千智海欧About Qianzhi Haiou

一家以「应用落地」为第一目标的 AI 公司。An AI company with deployment as its first priority.

千智海欧（青岛）人工智能科技有限公司成立于 2021 年，位于山东青岛高新区——这里聚集了众多人工智能与软件企业。我们从创立起就把自己定位为一家「应用落地型」的 AI 公司。

Founded in 2021 and based in the Qingdao Hi-Tech Zone, Shandong, Qianzhi Haiou has, from day one, positioned itself as a deployment-first AI company—in a region dense with AI and software talent.

我们关心的问题很朴素：人工智能技术如何真正进入业务、产生价值？为此，我们把能力拆成可交付的软件、算法与数据服务，而不是停留在 Demo 与 PPT。

Our question is simple: how can AI truly enter business and create value? We break capabilities into deliverable software, algorithms, and data services—not demos or slide decks.

从人工智能应用软件开发，到算法、大数据与物联网应用，我们围绕同一条主线工作——让技术在企业里跑起来、用得上、可度量。

From AI application software to algorithms, big data, and IoT, we work one thread: making technology run, stay usable, and stay measurable inside the enterprise.

技术理念·OUR APPROACH

我们怎么做How We Work

几条贯穿项目的朴素原则，用来判断「这件事值不值得做」。A few plain principles that guide whether something is worth doing.

01

务实落地

不追热点。先看业务能不能用、值不值得用，再决定投入。

We don't chase hype. We ask whether a use case is usable and worthwhile before investing.

02

数据驱动

用真实数据与反馈迭代效果，而不是凭直觉或演示文稿下结论。

We iterate on real data and feedback, not intuition or pitch decks.

03

可控可解释

在关键场景保留人工可介入与结果可追溯，让 AI 可被信任。

In critical scenarios we keep humans in the loop and results auditable, so AI earns trust.

04

工程优先

能稳定跑起来的方案，优先于看起来漂亮却落不了地的方案。

A solution that runs reliably beats one that looks good but can't ship.
